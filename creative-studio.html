The idea

“Brands need meaning, not just visuals.”

The creative premise connects strong aesthetics with informed, culturally relevant brand decisions.

01 / Positioning

A personal creative studio

A self-initiated studio identity for independent marketing and branding practice, framed around the gap between visual appeal and strategic meaning. The original brand-guidelines presentation describes creative work for personal brands, startups, service concepts, campaigns and business projects.

Guiding question: How can a visual identity express both artistic experimentation and analytical credibility?

02 / Identity explorations

A bilingual typographic direction

The 16-page guidelines explore a slender editorial Latin wordmark, Arabic lettering studies, geometric patterning, soft neutral backgrounds, and bolder condensed typography for statements.
